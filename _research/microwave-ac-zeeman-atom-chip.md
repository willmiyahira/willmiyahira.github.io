Microwave AC Zeeman potentials offer a route toward trapping and
manipulating ultracold atoms using near-field microwave radiation.

This project explores the design and implementation of microwave
atom chips based on microstrip transmission lines. My work involved
modeling microwave current distributions and calculating the resulting
AC Zeeman potentials, with particular attention to the effects of
microwave phase, chip geometry, and the proximity effect.

The broader goal is to develop flexible atom-chip platforms capable
of producing tailored trapping potentials for ultracold atoms.
