I am a postdoctoral researcher working with the [NL-eEDM](https://www.eedm.nl/) collaboration to make a precision measurement of the electron's electric dipole moment (eEDM).
I received my PhD from the College of William & Mary working in the [Ultra-cold Atom Lab](https://saaubi.people.wm.edu/ResearchGroup/index.html) of Dr. Seth Aubin, where I investigated the use of RF and microwave fields for the trapping and manipulation of atoms on an atom chip.
You can learn more about my work under the Research tab.
